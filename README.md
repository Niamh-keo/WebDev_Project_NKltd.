# WebDev_Project_NKltd.
This is a brief description of my project. 
I have been asked to make a website by a client named Lucas Snow. It will be an online website for snow trails travel agency.
a website all about snowboarding, winter sports, and exploring the coolest snow covered destinations. The website will be exciting and dynamic, with a few stories from guests.
The Website will have different pages with unique features. It will have a main page and an about page. The client themselves is a big fan of winter sports and snowboarding. The website will be designed to truly capture the true spirit of winter sports and snow boarding and just snow covered get aways.
this website will People who enjoy sports, who enjoy holidays with activity, people who prefer the cold weather rather than hot weather. Adrenaline seekers, and people who want to see the world from different perspectives.
The main components of my website will be different pages with titles of Accommodation, Resorts, guests views and personal stories from the resort, a page where you are able to contact the agency, a page for pictures of the different resorts, a page that can show you flights, an engaging website with clear user interface
